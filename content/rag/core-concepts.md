## Projects, Tasks, and Time Entries

Worklog Studio's data model has three core concepts:

- **Project**: a top-level container for related work, with a status of open, done, or archived.
- **Task**: belongs to a project, has its own title and description, and a status of open, done, or archived.
- **Time Entry**: a recorded span of time (start, optional end) that can be linked to a project and task, and can carry a free-text comment. A running time entry has no end time yet; a stopped one does.

This model is intentionally simple — no client/billing fields, no team assignment — matching the product's focus on solo, personal time tracking rather than agency or team billing workflows.

## "Flow Score," "Deep Work," and Other Analytics Terms

Terms like "Flow Score," "Deep Work hours," and "Efficiency %" appear in the landing page's Focus Analytics preview, but they are illustrative placeholder concepts for a planned feature, not metrics the app currently computes. There's no real definition for these yet — when that feature is built, this content will be updated with the actual definitions.
