## Value Proposition

Worklog Studio is a minimal, distraction-free desktop time logger for professionals, developers, and creators who prioritize deep work and flow states. It avoids the high-friction, form-heavy UI of traditional time trackers — no project setup wizard, no mandatory categories or tags before you can start a timer.

## Target Audience

Worklog Studio is built for engineers, designers, and founders who want to understand where their time actually goes without breaking their concentration to log it. It's deliberately not aimed at agencies or teams that need invoicing, billable-rate tracking, or client reporting — those features are out of scope by design, not yet-missing.

## Platforms & Availability

Worklog Studio ships as a native desktop app for macOS and Windows. It's free to use today, with the source code public under the MIT license at github.com/vavilov2212/wl-studio.
