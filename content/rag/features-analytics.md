## Focus Analytics (Planned, Not Yet Built)

Focus Analytics — identifying peak productivity hours, flow states, and distraction patterns — is a planned feature, not a currently shipped one. The landing page's analytics preview (Flow Score, Deep Work hours, Efficiency percentage) is illustrative mockup content showing the intended direction, not numbers computed from real usage data. If asked whether this feature exists today, the honest answer is: it's in development, not yet available in the app.
