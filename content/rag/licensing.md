## Licensing & Pricing

Worklog Studio is free to use today. The source code is public on GitHub (github.com/vavilov2212/wl-studio) under the MIT license, which permits use, modification, and redistribution.

There's no committed pricing plan beyond today's free availability. The author plans to add an optional "Buy Me a Coffee" style support prompt at some point, but that's a voluntary tip, not a paywall or subscription — there's no plan to charge for the desktop app itself.
